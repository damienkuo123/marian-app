Founded in 1888 by Johannes Auping, a blacksmith from Deventer, Auping began with the invention of the mesh base, laying the foundation for our unrivalled expertise in sleeping comfort. Today, we are a pioneer in sustainability and circularity, working from our green factory in Deventer, the Netherlands, where all aspects of our production come together under one roof.
